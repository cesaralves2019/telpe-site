The maths

One contract a year pays for a decade of alerts.

A supplier on Pro pays €228 a year. One small council contract covers that many times over. The question isn't the price; it's whether you'd have seen the notice at all.

Billing questions

The small print, in large print.

What happens after the free digest?

Nothing, unless you ask. The free plan stays free. Reply UPGRADE whenever a daily alert would have paid for itself, and we'll send a payment link.

Do you charge per alert?

No. A flat monthly price, however many notices fit. If nothing fits in a week, you hear nothing and pay the same.

Can I cancel?

Any time, by reply. No contract, no notice period, no phone call.

Is there a UK price?

Not yet. UK sources are included in Pro for Irish suppliers who work across the water; a sterling plan for UK suppliers comes later.
