How it works

Three steps. The third one is the only one that takes time, and it's yours.

01 · UPLOAD

One photo from your phone.

Telpe reads the room: what's in it, where the light comes from, the palette, the rough dimensions.

02 · TALK

Say it like you'd say it to a designer.

"A greener sofa." "Open up the window side." "What would make it feel bigger?" Telpe answers with the change, in your room, and the reason.

03 · DECIDE

Compare, keep, send.

Every version is kept. Slide between before and after, pick the one that works, export it for the client or the showroom.

For designers and architects

The first three options for a client, before lunch.

The early stage of a project is where most hours go and least gets paid. Studio turns a site photo into options in minutes, keeps the measurements honest, and leaves the real design to you.

For furniture shops

Your catalogue, their photo. A customer sees the piece in their own room, at the right size, in the right light, and the question changes from "will it fit" to "which colour".
