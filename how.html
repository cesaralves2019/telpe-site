Sources

Read at the source, the day it's published.

eTenders (Ireland)The national portal. Every Irish public notice appears here first, including sub-threshold contracts that never reach TED.
Contracts Finder & Find a Tender (UK)England, Scotland and Wales, from the smallest council contract to above-threshold procurements.
TED (EU)Tenders Electronic Daily, for above-threshold notices across the EU and cross-checking.

Council sitesQuotations and low-value notices some councils publish only on their own pages.

Questions

The honest answers.

Is this the official eTenders?

No. Telpe Tenders is an independent service that reads the public portals and tells you what fits. You still register and bid on the portal itself; we link you straight to the notice.

How fast are the alerts?

Daily alerts go out the same day a notice is published, usually within hours. Irish notices appear on eTenders one to three days before they reach TED, so we read eTenders directly.

Why a chat and not email?

Because tradespeople and small businesses read WhatsApp and Telegram and leave email for later. A chat also lets you reply: SAVE to track, MORE for the next one, STOP to pause.

Do you write the bid for me?

No. We help you find the right ones and never miss a deadline. Writing the tender is your work, or your consultant's.

What do you do with my number?

Send you the alerts you asked for, nothing else. You can pause with STOP and delete everything we hold with DELETE, both by reply.

Can I cancel?

Any time, by reply. No contract, no notice period.
