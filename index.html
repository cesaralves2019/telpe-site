How it works

Three steps. You do the first one once.

01 · TELL US

What you sell, where, and how big.

Pick your sectors, your counties and the contract sizes you'd bid for. Add the words that matter in your trade. Two minutes.

02 · WE WATCH

Every notice, every day, at the source.

We read eTenders, Contracts Finder, Find a Tender and TED as they publish, including the small notices that never leave the Irish portal.

Questions

The honest answers.

Is this the official eTenders?

No. Telpe Tenders is an independent service that reads the public portals and tells you what fits. You still register and bid on the portal itself; we link you straight to the notice.

How fast are the alerts?

Daily alerts go out the same day a notice is published, usually within hours. Irish notices appear on eTenders one to three days before they reach TED, so we read eTenders directly.

Why WhatsApp and not email?

Because tradespeople and small businesses read WhatsApp and leave email for later. You can still get a weekly email digest if you prefer; the matching is the same.

Do you write the bid for me?

No. We help you find the right ones and never miss a deadline. Writing the tender is your work, or your consultant's.

What do you do with my number?

Send you the alerts you asked for, nothing else. You can pause with STOP and delete your data with DELETE, both by reply.

Can I cancel?

Any time, by reply. No contract, no notice period.
