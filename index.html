    Today

    Hundreds of public websites, each with its own menu. Ireland.gov puts one question in front of all of them.

    The State already publishes almost everything people need. The hard part is finding it. Ireland.gov starts from your question, not from the department, and takes you to the right official site.

    Ask. Read a guide you can check. Then do it on the official site.

    1

    Ask in your own words

    Type what you need, or pick what is happening in your life. No account, no menu to learn. The search understands everyday words, and a few in Portuguese too.

    2

    Read a guide you can check

    Each guide is written in plain language from what public bodies publish, and lists the official pages it was read from and the date. Where two official sources disagree, the guide says so.

    3

    Finish the taskofficial site

    The green button opens the official service: Passport Online, MyWelfare, Revenue, the NDLS. Nothing is filled in or sent from here.

    Private by design.

    Nothing you type leaves your device

    The search runs in your browser against the guides already loaded with the page.

    No account, no cookies

    There is nothing to sign in to and nothing is stored about you.

    No ads, no tracking

    No advertising and no analytics. The only outside request the page makes is for its typeface.

    Public sources only

    The guides summarise what public bodies publish. When a figure could not be confirmed on an official page, it is left out and the guide links to the page instead.
